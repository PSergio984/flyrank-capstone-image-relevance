title: "Gray Wolf Pack Dynamics"
---

Gray wolves hunt as a coordinated pack, relying on social hierarchy and endurance to bring down large prey. Their thick gray coats and amber eyes are iconic in rocky, mossy terrain. This article examines how Canis lupus communicates through howls, establishes alpha leadership, and traverses alpine forests in groups of six to ten, a stark contrast to the solitary red fox. The pack's synchronized movement across lichen-covered rock, with pups learning by trailing adults, distinguishes the wolf from the lone fox or the domestic husky caught on camera.
