title: "City Skyline at Dusk"
---

At dusk the city skyline becomes a silhouette of glass and steel against a violet sky. Office lights flicker on floor by floor, and traffic hums below. This urban portrait explores how density shapes culture, why historic buildings anchor neighborhoods even as towers rise, and what the view from a rooftop at blue hour reveals about human ambition. The skyline's repetition of vertical lines and reflective glazing is unmistakably urban: no mountain, forest, or animal belongs here; the correct image must show towers, not trees.
