title: "Alpine Sunrise Over Snow Peaks"
---

An alpine sunrise paints snow-covered peaks pink before the valley wakes. This photo essay follows the light as it creeps down sheer ridges, reflects in a glassy tarn, and finally touches the trailhead. We discuss the geology of folded alpine ranges, why the air feels thinner above 2,500 meters, and how to time your hike for the first golden hour. The alpine mountain dominates the frame: its serrated ridgeline, permanent snowfields, and shadowed couloirs tell a story of uplift and glacial carving that no forest or desert scene can replicate.
