title: "Brown Bear Habitat in the Wild"
---

Brown bears roam river valleys and dense forests, fishing for salmon where rapids churn white. Their massive shoulders and dish-shaped faces distinguish them from smaller mammals, and their claws leave deep furrows in muddy banks. This post maps the brown bear's range across Alaska and Yellowstone, its hyperphagia before hibernation, and how coastal bears grow larger than their inland grizzly cousins thanks to protein-rich salmon runs. We also note denning behavior, mother-cub bonds through the first spring, and why photographers prize the brown bear's amber gaze at river crossings during late summer.
