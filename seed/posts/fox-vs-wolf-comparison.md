title: "Red Fox vs Gray Wolf: Know the Difference"
---

This is still a red fox post at heart — celebrating Vulpes vulpes — but it earns its nuance by comparing the fox to its larger lookalikes. The gray wolf dwarfs the fox, hunting in packs while the fox hunts alone; the Siberian husky mimics the wolf's coat but remains a domestic dog. We spell out that the fox is smaller, red-orange with a white tail tip, and solitary, while the wolf is gray, pack-bound, and twice the weight. The correct illustration is the fox, never the wolf, even though this post names both.
