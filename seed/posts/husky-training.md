title: "Training Your Siberian Husky"
---

The Siberian husky is a high-energy working dog bred to pull sleds across Siberian snowfields. With striking blue eyes and a wolf-like double coat, it is often mistaken for its wild cousin the gray wolf. This guide covers why huskies need daily running, how positive reinforcement tames their stubborn independence, and what makes their howl so uniquely expressive compared to other dogs. Unlike the wolf, the husky seeks human partnership: it thrives on teamwork, tolerates handling, and curls its tail over its back, traits no wild canid shares.
