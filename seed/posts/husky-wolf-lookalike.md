title: "When a Husky Looks Like a Wolf"
---

Owners love that their Siberian husky looks like a gray wolf — the thick double coat, the erect ears, the lupine stare. Yet the husky is unmistakably a dog: it pulls sleds on command, craves human praise, and rarely howls without a cue. This post is about the husky, not the wolf, and why the resemblance is superficial. We compare the husky's blue eyes and curled tail to the wolf's amber gaze and straight tail, concluding that only the dog image fits this story.
