title: "Misty Forest Trail at Dawn"
---

A forest trail disappears into morning mist, flanked by mossy oaks and ferns. Sunlight shafts cut through the canopy, illuminating drifting pollen. This walk invites you to notice the understory, the way mist hangs in hollows, and why autumn transforms this temperate forest into a cathedral of amber and green. The trail is narrow, soft with leaf litter, and flanked by ferns that thrive in shade. We explain how forest ecosystems recycle nutrients through mycorrhizal networks, and why the hush of a misty trail feels so different from an alpine ridge or city street.
